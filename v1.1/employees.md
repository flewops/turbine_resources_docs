# Employees

Employee records, the personal HR dashboard, loans and salary advances, company asset custody, employee documents, announcements and the resignation-to-settlement process.

## HR dashboard

### Your HR day on one page

Open **Dashboard** from the home screen.

- **Top cards** — your payslips, pending timesheets, running contracts and approved leaves, with trend lines. Change the period with the **Today** menu; **Export** downloads the data.
- **Check In / Check Out** — starts and stops your working timer (see [Attendances](attendances.md)).
- **Profile card** — position, department, joining month and employee ID.
- **Charts** — attendance trend, monthly leave, joiners and leavers, department-wise leave and attrition rate.
- **Requests** — counts of your loan, salary advance and resignation requests.
- **Side panels** — upcoming events, documents expiring soon, reminders, to-dos, pending approvals, birthdays and announcements.

The dashboard counts your own records. HR officers see company-wide figures in each application's reporting menu.

## Employee records

### The employee form

Each employee has one record with work information (department, job position, manager, working hours, work location), private information, contracts, documents and the related HR requests through smart buttons. The manager set here drives approval routing for leave, expenses and loans.

## Loans and salary advance

### Request a loan

A loan is repaid in monthly instalments deducted from salary.

1. Open **Employees → Loans & Advances → Request for Loan** and click **New**.
2. Enter the amount, number of instalments and first payment date.
3. Click **Compute Installment** to see the schedule, then **Submit**.
4. Your manager approves; if finance approval is enabled, Finance approves next. The amount is paid from the bank and each instalment is deducted from your payslip.

After approval, a loan can be settled early, an instalment deferred, or the loan topped up from the loan form.

### Request a salary advance

An advance is a one-time amount recovered from the next payslip.

1. Open **Salary Advance** and click **New**; enter the amount and reason.
2. The form shows your monthly wage, the maximum allowed and what you can still take.
3. Click **Submit**. After approval, Accounts clicks **Register Payment**, and the advance is recovered from your next payslip.

The advance limit is a percentage of wage set by HR; a larger request is refused.

## Custody of company assets

### Request, return and renew

1. Open **Custody → Custody Management → Custody Request** and click **New**.
2. Pick the property (laptop, phone, tools, vehicle), the purpose and the return date, then **Send For Approval**.
3. When HR approves, the item is issued to you.
4. Click **Return** when you hand it back, or **Renew** to extend.

An item already issued to someone else cannot be requested until it is returned.

## Documents, announcements and reminders

### Employee self-service

| Feature | Where | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documents | Employee profile → **Documents**, or **Employees → Management → Documents** | Upload passport, licence or ID with issue and expiry dates; Turbine notifies before expiry |
| Announcements | **Announcements** and the dashboard | Read company notices; click **Acknowledge** when asked |
| Reminders | **Reminders** | HR-defined alerts such as document expiry or contract end |
| To-do | **To-do** | Personal tasks and notes |
| Discuss | **Discuss** | Team channels and direct messages |

## Resignation and exit

### From resignation to relieving

1. Open **Resignation → Resignation Request**, click **New**, give the reason and expected last day, then **Confirm**.
2. Your manager clicks **Manager Approve**, then HR clicks **HR Approve**. You can **Withdraw** before HR approval.
3. **Clearance** — IT, Finance and Admin each mark their item **Clear** or **Block** and note any dues.
4. HR computes and approves the **settlement**: pending salary less loan, advance and asset recoveries.
5. On the last day, HR clicks **Relieve Employee**. An exit interview can follow.

Settle any open loan before the last day so it is cleared in the final pay.

## Approvals for managers and HR

### Approval buttons by request

| Request | Approve with | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time off and allocations | **Approve** in Time Off → Management | Manager, then HR where required |
| Expense | **Approve** in Expenses → Expenses to Process | Manager |
| Loan | **Approve**, then **Finance Approve** if enabled | Manager / HR, Finance |
| Salary advance | **Approve**, then **Register Payment** | HR, Accounts |
| Custody | **Approve** / **Refuse**; later **Renewal Approval** | HR |
| Announcement | **Approve** / **Refuse** | HR |
| Resignation | **Manager Approve**, **HR Approve**, **Approve Settlement**, **Relieve Employee** | Manager, HR |

## Administration

### Set-up

- Departments, job positions and managers on every employee.
- Loan and advance accounts and journals (loan receivable account, advance account, payment journal) per company.
- Advance limit as a percentage of wage.
- Custody properties and their approvers.
- Clearance departments for exit.
- Document types and expiry reminders.

## Troubleshooting

### Common issues

| Message or problem | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The requested amount exceeds the remaining eligible amount" | Advance above your limit | Ask for less, or request a loan |
| "You must enter Loan Receivable account and journal" | Accounting fields missing on the loan | Finance fills them, then approves |
| "Please configure the Payment Journal and Employee Advance Account" | Accounting fields missing on the advance | Finance fills both fields |
| "Custody is not available now" | The item is issued to someone else | Wait until it is returned |
| My request is not on the dashboard | The dashboard counts only your own records | Check the application itself |
