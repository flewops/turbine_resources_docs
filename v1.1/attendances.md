# Attendances

Check in and check out from any screen or a shared kiosk; Turbine adds up worked hours for timesheets, leave and payroll.

## Recording attendance

### Check in and out

1. Click **Check In** on the HR dashboard, or the clock icon in the top bar of any screen.
2. At the end of the day, click **Check Out**.
3. Your hours appear in **Attendances → My Attendances**.

Check-in works from a phone browser, so field staff can record attendance on site.

### Kiosk mode

At a shared screen at the gate or shop floor, open **Kiosk Mode**. Employees scan their badge or pick their name (with PIN if required) to check in and out.

Good to know: if you forget to check out, Attendance can close open check-ins automatically after a time set by HR. Ask HR to correct the record.

## For managers and HR

### Review attendance

- **Attendances → Overview** shows everyone's attendance by day or week.
- Group by employee, department or date to review late arrivals and missing check-outs.
- Correct an entry by opening it and editing the check-in or check-out time; the change is logged.

## Administration

### Settings

| Setting | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Kiosk mode and PIN | Badge or PIN identification at shared screens |
| Automatic check-out | Closes forgotten check-ins after a set number of hours |
| Working hours | Resource calendar per employee, used to compare worked and expected hours |

### Access rights

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Check in and out; see own attendance |
| Attendance Officer | See and correct the team's attendance |
| Attendance Administrator | Configure kiosk and settings |
