# Documents

A register of company licences and certificates — trade licence, factory licence, pollution consents, ISO and BIS certificates — with validity dates, automatic expiry and renewal follow-up.

## Where to find it

### Opening the register

Go to **Settings → Users & Companies → Documents → Documents**. You can also open your company (**Settings → Users & Companies → Companies**) and click the **Documents** smart button or the **Document History** tab.

Access to this menu is given by your administrator.

## Document states

### Draft, confirmed and expired

| State | Meaning | Editable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Entered but not yet checked | Yes |
| Confirm | Checked and active; tracked for expiry | No — locked |
| Expire | Validity date has passed (set automatically) | No |

Only confirmed documents are tracked for expiry. A draft document never expires and never sends a reminder.

## Working with documents

### Add a document

1. Open **Documents** and click **New**.
2. Enter the **Title**, for example "Trade Licence 2026–27".
3. Pick the **Document Type**. If it is missing, ask your administrator to add it.
4. Upload the scanned file in **Document** (one file per record).
5. Enter the **Issue Date** and **Expiry Date**. Leave Expiry Date empty for documents that never expire, such as a GST registration certificate.
6. Add remarks in the **Note** tab.
7. Click **Confirm** when the details are checked. The record is locked.

Turbine assigns a reference (for example CD/00007), sets you as **Responsible** and links the document to the current company. If the expiry date is before the issue date, Turbine shows "Expiry Date must be after Issue Date."

### Find documents

- Filter by **Draft**, **Confirm** or **Expire**.
- Group by **Document Type** or **State** to review licences by category.

### Renew a document

1. Create a **new** document with the renewed file and new dates, and confirm it.
2. On the old document, post a chatter note such as "Renewed — see CD/000xx".

### Follow-ups

Use the chatter to log notes, attach supporting letters and schedule activities — for example "Apply for renewal" 45 days before expiry.

## Good practice

### Do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Confirm every document after checking it | Leave documents in Draft — they are never tracked |
| Keep one record per document version | Overwrite an old licence with a new file |
| Fill the expiry date for anything that lapses | Delete expired documents — they are your audit history |

## Administration

### Set-up

- Create document types: Trade Licence, Factory Licence, Consent to Operate, Fire NOC, ISO Certificate, BIS Licence, GST Registration, MSME (Udyam) Certificate and others you hold.
- Give access to the people who maintain the register.
- In multi-company databases, enter each company's documents with that company selected.

Employee documents (passports, licences, ID) are kept on the employee record — see [Employees](employees.md#documents-announcements-and-reminders).
