# Recruitment

Job positions published on your website, an applicant pipeline from application to signed offer, standard interview forms and one-click employee creation.

## Hiring flow

### From job opening to new employee

1. Create the job in **Recruitment** and click **Publish** to show it on your website's Jobs page.
2. Applications arrive from the website or by email to the job's alias.
3. Drag applicant cards through the stages: New → Qualification → First Interview → Second Interview → Contract Proposal → Contract Signed.
4. Schedule interviews from the applicant with **Meeting**; invitations go to the candidate and interviewers.
5. Use **Refuse** with a reason when a candidate is not taken forward; a refusal email can be sent.
6. At Contract Signed, click **Create Employee**. The employee form opens pre-filled; review and save.

## Interview forms

### Standardised evaluation

Attach an **Interview Form** (a survey) to the job position. From the applicant, click **Send Interview** to email it, or fill it in during the interview. The result is stored on the applicant, so every candidate is scored on the same criteria. Build forms in [Surveys](surveys.md).

## Reports

### Recruitment analysis

**Recruitment → Reporting** shows applicants by job, stage, source and refusal reason, and time spent in each stage.

## Administration

### Set-up

- Job positions with department, recruiter, interviewers and email alias.
- Stages and refusal reasons.
- Email templates for acknowledgement, interview invitation and refusal.
- Website Jobs page and application form.

### Access rights

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Interviewer | See and evaluate applicants for assigned jobs |
| Recruitment Officer | Manage applicants and jobs |
| Recruitment Administrator | Configure stages, templates and settings |
