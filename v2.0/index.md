## Getting started

### What is Turbine?

Turbine is a fully integrated **business operating system** that unifies finance, sales, supply chain, HR, marketing, services, and productivity apps in one platform — hosted on AWS and managed for you.

### How to begin

1. **Book a demo** — map your processes and priority apps with our team.
2. **Scope implementation** — one-time setup for configuration, data migration, and training.
3. **Go live** — your instance is provisioned, secured, and maintained by us.
4. **Add users & apps** — scale with a clear per-user monthly subscription.

## What's new in v2.0
