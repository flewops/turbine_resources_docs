# CRM

Track every lead and opportunity from first contact to signed deal.

## Getting started

### Set up your pipeline

1. Open **CRM** from the app menu.
2. Rename the default stages to match your sales process.
3. Invite your salespeople and assign them to a sales team.

![The CRM pipeline board](images/crm-pipeline.jpg "Pipeline board with stages and opportunities")

### Capture leads

Leads arrive from website forms, email and live chat. Each lead gets an owner and a next activity automatically.

When a deal is won, turn it into a quotation — see [Send a quotation](sales.md#quotations) in the Sales guide.

## Working the pipeline

### Stages and probabilities

| Stage | Default probability | Typical next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | 10% | Qualify the need |
| Qualified | 30% | Book a demo |
| Proposition | 60% | Send a quotation |
| Won | 100% | Hand over to Sales |

### Activities

- Schedule calls, meetings and emails on each opportunity.
- Overdue activities show in red on the board.
- *Tip:* use the **My Activities** filter every morning.

## What's new in v2.0

### Forecast view

Expected revenue now rolls up by month and salesperson.

#### How to open it

Go to **CRM → Reporting → Forecast** and group by *Expected closing*.
