# Sales

Quotations, orders and invoicing — from quote to cash.

## Quotations

### Send a quotation

Pick products, apply the customer's pricelist and send the quote for online signature. Leads usually come from [CRM](crm.md#capture-leads).

![A quotation ready to send](images/sales-quotation.jpg "Quotation with optional products")

### Optional products (new in v2.0)

Add optional lines that customers can include themselves when they open the quote online.

## Orders and invoicing

### Confirm and deliver

When the customer signs, the order is confirmed and a delivery is created in [Inventory](inventory.md).

### Invoicing policy

| Policy | Invoice is based on |
| --- | --- |
| Ordered quantities | What the customer ordered |
| Delivered quantities | What was actually shipped |

Invoices post to [Accounting](accounting.md) automatically.
